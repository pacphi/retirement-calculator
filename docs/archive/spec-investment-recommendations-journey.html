2 · End-to-end journey

1

Couple works through steps 1–10 as today

Ages, retirement timing, pension, spending — nothing about this feature changes those steps. Their answers there are what step 11 reads silently: current age, retirement age, and which year retirement starts.

2

Step 11 — Investments new step

They set a risk tolerance, optionally attest to accredited-investor status, and list their accounts (401k, IRA, taxable, HSA…) with balances. Nothing here is required to generate a report — skipping it just means the new report section shows generic guidance with no account table.

3

"Generate report"

Same button as today. Behind it, investmentAdvisor.js derives their phase from step 3's ages (accumulation / decumulation / both, for the transition year) and computes an allocation from the risk × phase rule table.

4

Report → Investment Recommendations new section

Placed right after Portfolio. Shows the phase-appropriate fund table with citations and signup links, then a per-account breakdown if they entered accounts in step 11 — or a plain allocation summary if they didn't.

5

Print / export

Section renders in the existing ReportDocument print path unchanged — same disclaimer discipline as every other section, just with an extra planning-grade banner specific to investment content.
